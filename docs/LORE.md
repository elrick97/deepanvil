## Guests from other realms (v0.5–0.6)

Claude's crew stay dwarves. Other providers arrive as visiting guilds, so you can see at a glance who is working:

| Guild | Provider | Look | Flavour |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gnomes of the Clockwork Guild** | OpenAI (Codex) | Brass goggles, wind-up tools, tiny steam backpacks | Precise tinkerers; their hammers tick |
| **Elves of the Twin Stars** | Google (Gemini) | Tall, silver-green robes, star-lit tools; arrive in pairs | Graceful and quick; work by starlight |
| **Wandering journeymen** | Anything else (local or open models) | Humans with travel packs | Jacks of all trades, paid in stew |

Guests take a free anvil; the anvil gets their guild's banner while they work.

## Titles and achievements (Muninn's chronicle)

- **Rank by merged pieces:** Apprentice (0) → Journeyman (5) → Smith (20) → Master Smith (60) → Runesmith (150).
- **Feats:**
  - *First Light*: the first quest merged.
  - *Thrice-Quenched*: merged after two failed tests.
  - *Odin's Favoured*: 10 pieces approved first try.
  - *Featherweight*: a fix under 10 lines.
  - *Mender of Main*: turned main green again.
  - *Night Owl*: merged after midnight.
  - *Bellringer*: 25 permission requests.
- **Hall trophies:**
  - every 10 merges hangs a shield on the wall;
  - each project gets a banner;
  - milestones unlock decor (a second garden, a dragon-skull trophy, a tavern corner).
